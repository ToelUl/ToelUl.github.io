# Research

My work sits at the interface of theoretical physics, computational physics, and machine learning. A recurring theme is to use structure—symmetry, geometry, locality, constraints, and scaling—to make difficult physical problems more interpretable or computationally tractable.

## Quantum many-body physics and critical phenomena

I am interested in critical behavior, exactly solvable many-body models, lattice systems, and geometric quantities that characterize quantum states and responses. The goal is to connect formal structures to concrete observables and scalable numerical tests.

Related work: [Flow Matching–based Monte Carlo initialization]({{ '/publications/2026-flow-matching/' | relative_url }}) · [Solvable Quantum Many-Body Models](https://github.com/ToelUl/solvable-quantum-many-body-models) · [quantum-simulation](https://github.com/ToelUl/quantum-simulation)

## Machine learning for physical systems

I develop and study generative and equivariant learning methods for physics, including Flow Matching and lattice-gauge-equivariant neural networks. The emphasis is on models whose inductive biases reflect physical structure rather than treating physical data as generic high-dimensional arrays.

Related work: [Flow-to-Field](https://github.com/ToelUl/Flow-to-Field) · [Lattice-gauge-equivariant-CNN](https://github.com/ToelUl/Lattice-gauge-equivariant-CNN) · [Fluxonium parameter characterization]({{ '/publications/2025-fluxonium/' | relative_url }})

## Geometric and mathematical physics

I am interested in geometric formulations of field theory and gravity, including constrained Hamiltonian systems, ADM geometrodynamics, connection variables, gauge structure, and related differential-geometric ideas.

Related work: [From ADM Geometrodynamics to Ashtekar Connection Dynamics]({{ '/publications/2026-adm-ashtekar/' | relative_url }}) · [Einstein-tensor-calculator](https://github.com/ToelUl/Einstein-tensor-calculator)

## Scientific computation

My computational work includes GPU-accelerated Monte Carlo, numerical simulation of lattice systems, tensor and linear-algebra utilities, and reproducible PyTorch-based research workflows.

Related work: [Lattice-Field-MCMC-GPU](https://github.com/ToelUl/Lattice-Field-MCMC-GPU) · [EfficientSVD](https://github.com/ToelUl/EfficientSVD) · [ncon-torch](https://github.com/ToelUl/ncon-torch)
