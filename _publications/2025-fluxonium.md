This work develops a transfer-learning-based workflow for automatic characterization of fluxonium-qubit Hamiltonian parameters from spectroscopic data. The method uses simulated spectra for training and combines machine-learning estimates with automated fitting, including tests with noisy or incomplete spectral inputs.
